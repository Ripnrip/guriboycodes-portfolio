- **AI IDE Setup & Workflows → cosmos** (not agent0). The shortlist lists `Ripnrip/ai-config`, `ai-config-brain`, and `/Users/admin/Documents/Developer/ai-ide-setup` as cosmos-bundle evidence — the AI-IDE/dev-stack story is the personal face of the "unified LLM gateway + AI dev stack" bundle. Agent0's evidence is the agent-zero-macOS/openclaw-career line, which never mentions ai-ide-setup. Side effect: cosmos inherits the tile's Ghibli art (screenshot ✅). Note: the tile links `Ripnrip/ai-ide-setup`, which the audit marks **private** — another dead public link to fix or remove.
- **Hylios → ethereal.** data.js calls Hylios "a published iOS game in the Ethereal Dimension universe"; it also supplies the bundle's App Store link.
- **Ethereal Search Showcase → ethereal.** Same universe/brand (etherealsearch-showcase + EtherealDimension-Search repos); keeps the search work attached to the flagship instead of orphaned.
- **Rosicrucian Vowel Sounds, Rosicrucian Parenting, Voice Practice Hub → rosicrucian.** Same community ecosystem; the two App Store apps turn the bundle from "a RAG demo" into "a shipped product family," and Voice Practice Hub is the browser companion to the vowel-practice work.
- **Flow → flow**, **Agentic News Transformer + Zai Vision Suite → ant-zai**, **Gurduwara Langar Game → hackathons**, **Artful Archives → artful**: direct one-to-one mappings implied by the shortlist.
- **Left unabsorbed (pure-fun standalone tiles): Audio Dialogues, Cycles of Fate, Geometric Graph Design.** Audio Dialogues is generic voice-AI play rather than Rosicrucian-branded; Cycles of Fate and Geometric Graph are generative-art one-offs. They work better as small joy tiles than stretched into a bundle.
- **Repo-level folds (not sideProjects):** `observability-web-dashboard` + `observability-swift-client` → vireo (per shortlist near-miss note); SuperProductivityDynamicIsland local checkout → flow (its git origin *is* `Ripnrip/Flow`); Agentic SEO + pens-eeg-game → hackathons proof rail.
